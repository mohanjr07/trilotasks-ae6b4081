TaskFlow – Account & data deletion

App: TaskFlow · Developer: Trilo Automation Private Limited

How to request deletion

Send an email from your work email address to saravanan@triloautomation.com with the subject "TaskFlow account deletion request" and your name and employee ID. We will confirm your identity and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, email, photo, phone, employee ID).
  • Your push-notification tokens and notification history.
  • Personal notes and documents you uploaded that are not company records.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Company work records you were part of – tasks, approved forms, attendance, leave, overtime and payment records with their bills and receipts – may be kept for up to 8 years where required for accounting, tax, audit or legal reasons. These are kept only for those purposes and are no longer linked to an active account.
