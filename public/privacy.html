TaskFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TaskFlow ("the app") is an internal work-management app operated by Trilo Automation Private Limited ("we", "us"), PK 18, Phase V, Guindy Industrial Estate, Chennai, India. It is used by our employees and authorised staff to manage tasks, attendance, leave, forms, payment requests and related work. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

1. Who can use the app

Accounts are created by our administrators for employees and authorised staff only. There is no public sign-up. Anyone can download the app, but it cannot be used without an account issued by Trilo Automation.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details – name, work email address, role, department, employee ID and profile photo (if added).
  • Work records – tasks, comments, projects, notes, leave and permission requests, overtime entries, digital forms and their approvals, and payment requests with the bills and receipts you upload.
  • Attendance records – punch-in and punch-out times received from the company's attendance machine. The app does not collect fingerprints or any biometric data.
  • Files you upload – photos, PDFs and documents attached to forms, payment requests or documents.
  • Device information for notifications – a push-notification token so we can send you work alerts.
  • Basic technical data – sign-in sessions and error information needed to keep the service working.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, call logs, SMS, or advertising identifiers, and it does not contain advertising.

3. How we use the information

  • To run the company's day-to-day work: assigning tasks, approvals, attendance, leave, overtime and payments.
  • To send in-app, push and email notifications about your work (for example, an approval waiting for you).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing

Information is visible inside the company only to the people who need it for their role (for example, your manager, approvers, administrators and accounts). We use trusted service providers to operate the app – Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in), Cloudflare (website hosting), Google Firebase (push notifications) and an email delivery provider – who process data only on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is protected by sign-in and role-based permissions, and uploaded files are stored privately and opened through short-lived secure links.

6. Data retention

Work records are kept for as long as they are needed for business, legal and accounting purposes. When an employee leaves, their account is deactivated; records required for company or legal reasons may be retained.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can view and update your profile in the app. To request correction or deletion of your account and personal data, see Account & data deletion or email saravanan@triloautomation.com. You can turn off push notifications in your phone's settings at any time.

8. Children

The app is intended for employees and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
